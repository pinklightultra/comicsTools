## Art notes for this layout

- Default **3–4 panels**; **5** only for Arcade intro and Pringles humiliation.  
- One **wide** beat every 2–3 pages (aerial, venue, trunk, raccoon hood, lot button).  
- Pages **1–4** stay your finished samples; rebuild **5–30** in that breathing room.  
- When compositing from pencil crops: prefer story order over “use every panel” — leftovers feed #2 boards.
